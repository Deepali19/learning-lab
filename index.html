How it works

1

Pick a project

Find your child's grade and subject. Each project has an interactive model to explore together.

2

Follow the steps

Every step tells you what to show, what to ask, what to say, and what to try if your child gets stuck.

3

Tick off progress

Mark steps as done. Your progress is saved in this browser, so you can continue another day.

4

Print if you like

Every project has a printable parent guide for learning away from the screen.
